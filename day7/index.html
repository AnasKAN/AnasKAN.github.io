What moves each batch

The student follows −lr · (∇L + noise). The noise is the minibatch: each batch sees different images and crops, so its gradient points a bit off from the true downhill direction. The violet arrow is the pull the teacher feels toward the student.

Why the teacher ends lower

The teacher is a running average of every place the student has been. Random zig-zags cancel out in that average, while a direction the student keeps returning to accumulates. That is why the teacher's line is smoother and usually sits under the student's once they reach the basin.

Where the cartoon is wrong

In real DINO the loss is cross-entropy against the teacher's own output, so the landscape itself shifts as the teacher moves, and it lives in millions of dimensions. Here the terrain is fixed and the small "pull toward teacher" stands in for that target.
