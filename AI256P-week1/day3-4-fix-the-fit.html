QUESTION 2 OF 2

Notice the mirror

Both questions had the same options. Almost every fix for overfitting causes underfitting when overdone, and the other way round: you are moving one dial between bias and variance.

Two options fix neither: gradient clipping keeps training stable (no exploding gradients), and mixed precision makes it faster. We used both in Part II for stability and speed, not to change the fit.
