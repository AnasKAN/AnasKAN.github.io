03Debrief: how to read them

GRAPH 1

Underfitting · high bias

Both curves flatten early at a high error and stay close together. The model can't even fit its own training data: too simple, too frozen, or not trained long enough.

GRAPH 2

Fitted · good fit

Both errors fall and settle low, with a small, stable gap. Validation tracks training. This is what we want.

GRAPH 3

Overfitting · high variance

Training error keeps dropping toward zero, but validation error bottoms out and then climbs. After that point the model is memorising the training set. Early stopping keeps the checkpoint at the lowest validation error.

Back to our notebook: the summary table calls Phase 1 (frozen backbone, ~40% accuracy) bias-limited, like Graph 1. Unfreezing Stage 4 gives the model more capacity, so the risk shifts to variance: watch for Graph 3. Exercise 4 asks what to do about each.
