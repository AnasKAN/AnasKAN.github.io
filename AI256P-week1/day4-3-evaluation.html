Measuring vs deploying

Measure mAP at confidence ≈ 0.001, because it integrates the whole precision–recall curve. Use the high thresholds only for deployment.

Size buckets (COCO)

small < 32² px · medium < 96² px · large above that. Slice your misses by size before you fix anything.

Thresholds are a cost decision

Recall first when a miss is expensive. Precision first when every alert costs attention. Otherwise use the F1-optimal point, and always quote the threshold next to P / R.
