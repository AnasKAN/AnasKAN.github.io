02Glossary

  • Bounding boxA rectangle marking where an object is: 4 numbers plus a class. VOC, COCO and YOLO write it differently.§2
  • YOLOOne-stage detector. A grid of cells predicts boxes and classes in a single forward pass, fast enough for video.§3
  • Confidence scoreObjectness × class probability. A good ranking, not a calibrated probability. You threshold it.§3–4
  • NMSNon-maximum suppression. Keep the highest-scoring box, delete its overlapping near-duplicates, repeat. Pure geometry after the forward pass.§4
  • IoUIntersection over union: overlap area ÷ combined area. Symmetric, scale-free, in [0, 1]. It decides whether a prediction matches a ground-truth box.§5
  • mAPAP (area under one class's precision–recall curve), averaged over classes. mAP50 uses IoU 0.5; mAP50-95 averages 0.50 to 0.95.§5
  • DETR / RT-DETRTransformer detector that predicts a fixed set of boxes. No NMS at inference, and constant latency.§7
  • Hungarian matchingHow DETR trains: each prediction is paired with at most one ground-truth box, so a duplicate is punished by the loss itself.§7
