About

I'm an AI Engineer based in Saudi Arabia. I like turning research ideas into systems that work in the real world.

At Nabrah.AI I started as an AI Research Engineer intern and joined full-time as an AI Engineer, working on audio processing and AI research & development. Before that, at the Institute of Hajj and Umrah Research, I modeled the scheduling of pilgrim movement (Tafweej) as an optimization problem. I earned a first-class honours degree in Computer Science from Umm Al-Qura University, where I also led the university's Artificial Intelligence team. In 2025 I completed the AI Summer School at the University of Oxford (Lady Margaret Hall) with an A+.
