EXERCISE 2 · POST-PROCESSING (§3.4)

Be the NMS.

The detector fired eight boxes on three workers and a traffic cone. Your job is to do exactly what nms() does in §3.4: cross out every box that does not survive.

Work with the person next to you. One of you reads the confidences out loud, the other crosses boxes out.

01The algorithm

STEP 1

Confidence filter

Drop every box with confidence below τconf = 0.25.

STEP 2

Per class, best first

Sort the boxes of one class by confidence and keep the best one.

STEP 3

Suppress, then repeat

Delete same-class boxes with IoU > τNMS = 0.5 against the kept box. Repeat with the next survivor.

03Debrief: the trace

  1. Confidence filter

    H5 (0.18, the traffic cone) is below 0.25 and never reaches NMS. 7 boxes remain.

  2. Helmets: H1 0.91 → H2 0.84 → H3 0.62 → H4 0.47

    Keep H1. It suppresses H2 (IoU 0.67) and H3 (IoU 0.63). H4 does not overlap H1 at all, so it is the next survivor.

  3. Heads: D1 0.88 → D3 0.80 → D2 0.55

    Keep D1. It suppresses D2 (IoU 0.52, just over 0.5). D3 overlaps D1 by only 0.09. It is a real neighbour in a crowd, so it survives.

  4. Result: 4 boxes. H1 · H4 · D1 · D3

    8 candidates → 7 after the confidence filter → 4 after NMS. It's the same funnel as §3.4, where 8,400 → 48 → 5.

Did you spot the odd one? Worker 2 now has two boxes.

NMS is class-wise: a head box never suppresses a helmet box, even at IoU 0.89. So worker 2 is reported as helmet and bare head at the same time.

That is the lab's ⭐ optional class-agnostic NMS (agnostic_nms=True): a head is either helmeted or bare, never both, so the two classes may suppress each other. On our validation set it barely moved mAP50-95 (0.4026 → 0.3992), so measure before you switch it on.

τNMS too low

In a crowd, real neighbours (like D3) overlap. A low threshold deletes them, and recall drops.

τNMS too high

Duplicates (like H2, H3) survive and every one becomes a false positive.

So the lab sweeps it

§6 step 1: try τNMS = 0.3 … 0.9 on the validation split at τconf = 0.001, and keep the best mAP50.
